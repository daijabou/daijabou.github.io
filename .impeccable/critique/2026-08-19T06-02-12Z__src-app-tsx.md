## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored in three places, category-interchangeable elsewhere. Genuine authorship: `git log --career` Experience treatment, the real command registry in `terminalCommands.ts`, status-bar-as-OS-taskbar. The page skeleton is a 2019 one-page-dev-portfolio template; the terminal identity is applied via four skin operations (font swap, green palette, headings renamed to commands, CRT filter).

Tell 1: a terminal never center-aligns its output, yet `$ cat about.md` is followed by centered ragged prose and `$ ls ./skills` produces a comma-joined centered sentence typed one char at a time. Commands are captions bolted onto a conventional layout, not the mechanism producing it. Hero and Skills share the same wrapper.

Tell 2: the strongest asset is invisible. The AI terminal (PRODUCT.md: "the proof of the claim") sits behind an unlabeled 48x48 icon, then behind knowing to type `ask`. Complete tab-focusable set: hero CTA, 3 skill tabs, 3 form fields, send, mailto, terminal launcher, 5 nav links. A visitor who never types cannot reach the GitHub profile at all.

Tell 3: the loudest type (VT323 128px + glow) is spent on "Hello, I'm Michael". "Software Engineer" appears nowhere in the first viewport.

Deterministic scan: detector install DEGRADED (no node_modules in the skill dir; regex fallback, contrast/custom-properties unevaluated). A full-fidelity scratchpad copy with htmlparser2/css-select/css-tree/domutils returned the identical result, so the count is trustworthy.

- CLI: 1 finding, `codex-grid-background` (advisory) at `src/index.css:110`. Real but low-value: `.grid-bg` applied identically to 5 of 5 sections as generic backdrop.
- In-page overlay: 18 element groups / 20 findings. `dark-glow` x17, `blinking-cursor` x1, `pulsing-dot` x1, `codex-grid-background` x1.
- False positives: `dark-glow` largely invalid (zero-offset phosphor glow is the binding aesthetic) EXCEPT 6 of 17 on 14px company lines where glow smears glyphs. `blinking-cursor` invalid. `pulsing-dot` REAL - `animate-ping` is a generated-UI tell the CRT theme does not motivate.

## Overall Impression

The engineering underneath is better than the design on top. `terminalCommands.ts` is a real system and the reason a technical visitor believes the rest. The page it is attached to hides it, dims its output below readability, and asks a recruiter to understand `cat`. Biggest opportunity: there is no decision layer between Tailwind and the components.

## What's Working

1. `src/lib/terminalCommands.ts` is a real system, not a switch statement - `Command` interface with optional `complete`, aliases, usage; discriminated `OutputLine`/`CommandAction` unions so output is typed data; `longestCommonPrefix` drives genuine completion; prefix-based did-you-mean. The positioning made structural.
2. The `git log --career` Experience treatment. `(HEAD -> main)` on the current role is a real semantic mapping (chronology to commit history). The one place the metaphor generates the layout.
3. The global focus ring: `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--term-phosphor); outline-offset: 2px }` at `index.css:61`, verified on a real Tab press. Easy to lose in a black-on-black aesthetic; deliberately kept.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] A recruiter cannot screen the site, and a client cannot see evidence, without typing a command

Violates Product Principle 2 (metaphor is delivery mechanism, not gate). First viewport = name + looping generic tagline + one CTA to placeholder prose. GitHub/LinkedIn absent from the tab-focusable set. Skills default to the generic category and are gated behind ~3s of unskippable typewriter. No resume download.

Fix: role/stack/recency as static hero text styled as `whoami` output; GitHub + LinkedIn as persistent affordances in a real `<footer>` as `ls -la ~/links`; visible label on the terminal launcher (`>_ ask michael`) plus two clickable starter prompts; default Skills to `AI/LLM Tools` rendered instantly as a column listing.

Command: /impeccable layout, then /impeccable clarify

### [P1] Mobile navigation is five unlabeled 24x27px digits at ~1.8:1 contrast

`StatusBar.tsx:80` hides labels below `md`. Measured: 4.23:1 bare, 3.87:1 with scanlines, 1.78:1 under the vignette. The 36px bar caps items at 28px at any width (source-level, not responsive). Visible label and accessible name both reduce to a bare number.

Fix: keep labels at all widths (`home about skills work hi` fits 390px) or make the bar `snap-x` scrollable; raise bar to 44px; inactive chips to `phosphor/70` min; mask the vignette out of the status-bar band or restack above `z-[100]`.

Command: /impeccable adapt

### [P1] Decorative dimming has spread onto content; vignette lands on functional chrome

`text-term-text/50` at 2.63:1 carries `cd` confirmations, keybinding help, every error-recovery hint, and the duration line of every job in `experience` output. `phosphor/40` at 3.09:1 labels `Date:` on every card. `placeholder-term-phosphor/25` at 1.82:1. CRT vignette `z-[100]` sits above StatusBar `z-[90]` and console `z-[95]`. Main body token `text-term-text` is fine at 7.3:1 - damage confined to `/40` and `/50` at 11-12px.

Fix: three named tokens (`--text-primary` 7.5:1, `--text-secondary` phosphor/70, `--text-hint` floor 4.5:1); map `toneClass` onto them; raise `dim` /50 to /70; placeholders to phosphor/45; `mask-image` excluding the bottom 36px from the vignette.

Command: /impeccable colorize

### [P1] The prompt sigil has three fonts and eight colours; nothing shares a scale

The `$`/`~$`/`>` glyph renders in Share Tech Mono, VT323 and Noto Sans Mono across six locations in 8 colour treatments. Body prose at 14/18/20/24px for one role. Four `SectionHeading` bottom margins. Six container widths. Seven border values. Five transition durations. Hover is identical to selected on Skills tabs. All readable content is Noto Sans Mono - no readable content carries the identity.

## Minor Observations

- `cd <section>` silently does nothing when system animations are disabled: `scrollIntoView({behavior:'smooth'})` produced zero movement across 14 samples while `prefers-reduced-motion` was false, so the guard at `TerminalConsole.tsx:215` never fires. Needs a non-smooth fallback.
- `Typewriter.tsx` has zero reduced-motion handling; `index.css:118` only zeroes CSS durations, not JS timers. Both loops animate forever. PRODUCT.md requirement missed; WCAG 2.2.2 failure (no pause).
- Mobile console occupies 52% of the viewport with no scrim, hiding everything from `--name` down. `bottom-11` is layout-viewport-anchored while the input auto-focuses, so a software keyboard pushes it out of sight.
- Contact fields have accessible names of literally `--name`/`--email`/`--message`.
- Amber marks version control, time, AI mode and system notices. Magenta means glitch chroma, error state and Close-button hover, so hovering Close reads as an error.
- `SectionHeading`'s `align` prop is dead code.
- `Skills.tsx:11-13` nests `min-h-[50vh]` inside `h-screen` inside `h-screen`.
- `<title>` is bare "Michael Endaya"; no OG/Twitter meta; `public/logo.png` unused.
- Live Contentful copy typo: "used by thousand call center agents".
- PRODUCT.md correction needed: `LINKEDIN_URL` is now populated (uncommitted) and Contentful IS configured locally (Asurion, Realtair, Fourello render). The placeholder warning applies to `bio` and `taglines` only.
- `.impeccable/config.local.json` is committed and not gitignored.

## Questions to Consider

1. What if the terminal were not a widget on the page, but the page? Two parallel information systems currently duplicate each other and share nothing.
2. If a terminal never centers its output, what does it mean that four of five sections do? Would one left-aligned column with a real gutter make the identity stronger while fixing About, Skills and Contact at once?
3. PRODUCT.md says the AI assistant is the proof of the positioning. Why is it the least visible element? What if the hero tagline slot were its input?
4. What is the shell equivalent of reassurance? Could `$ send --now` return `-> queued / delivered to <email> / typical reply < 24h`?
5. Two co-equal audiences, one form, one voice. Should `./contact.sh` take `--role` vs `--project` so the metaphor solves the dual-audience problem instead of obscuring it?
